Student wellness prototype

IU GymPal

Campus rec apps often assume you already know the machines. GymPal is an AI-assisted companion for IU students who want a plan, a form check, and a reason to come back tomorrow.

Role
Interface design, research
Research
20+ IU student interviews
Platform
Mobile prototype
Tools
Figma

What students actually said

I interviewed more than twenty IU students about working out on campus. The pattern was consistent: motivation is fragile, gyms feel intimidating if you don’t already belong there, and generic fitness apps don’t know SRSC hours, student schedules, or the fact that midterms will wreck a streak.

The product promise became: make the next session obvious, show form without shame, and treat consistency as the metric — not aesthetics.

What I designed

  • A home that leads with today’s workout, past data, and the week — not a social feed.
  • Personalized recommendations (the “AI” layer) as a planner, not a chatbot occupying the screen.
  • Form tutorials as a support surface for people who would otherwise skip a movement they don’t know.
  • Streak and motivation copy that is direct. Students did not ask for a mascot. They asked not to lose the thread after a bad week.

What I would change

Looking at this with a colder eye: the home list and the bottom navigation repeat each other, icon weights don’t match, and the motivational panel fights the content for contrast. Those are real critique notes, and I would fix them before calling this production-ready. The research insight still holds — students need a campus-specific on-ramp, not another generic tracker.
