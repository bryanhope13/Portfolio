Approach

How a date with us goes.

  1. 01

    The conversation

    You tell us the room, the people, and the songs that are non-negotiable. We tell you what we need to make that night feel easy.

  2. 02

    The plan

    A simple timeline: ceremony, dinner, first dances, open floor. Must-plays and do-not-plays. No surprises for you or your coordinator.

  3. 03

    The night

    We arrive early, stay present, and adjust when the room asks for it. The goal is not a loud set. It is a night that feels like yours.
